Play Now

How to play

Tap a piece

Every group of cells is one piece, marked with its own arrows.

Follow its arrows

The arrows trace the exact path the piece would slide along.

Clear path, clean exit

If nothing blocks that path, the piece slides off the board.

Empty the board

Clear every piece before you run out of attempts to win.
